03 · The atelier

A souk, a table, a pair of hands

"A bouquet should smell like the morning it was bought."

Bouquet Fleurs has no shopfront and no window display — it is a small flower studio that moves with the season. At half past six, while the town is still cool, we walk to souk El Had with a wicker basket and choose whatever grows best that day: roses from the Souss plain, tuberose in late summer, gypsophila all year round.

Nothing sits in a fridge waiting to wilt. You order by WhatsApp or DM, we tie it the same morning, wrap it in red kraft, and it is at your door before the light turns.

We deliver across the whole of Tiznit — the medina, the centre, the newest quarters at the edge of town — and for weddings we cover every table in the city. There is no address to remember; the flowers come to you.
